# Collision, walkways and the SHOW COLLISION view (DONE12)

## What the player collides with

`StreamedFacilityView3D` builds one `BoxShape3D` per cell of wall, floor and ceiling, and **one box per solid prop**
(`collider_size`, centred on the prop's placement offset, rotated by its yaw). Room doors are openable gaps. Decor (rugs,
wall art, ceiling lights, anything wall/ceiling/floor-dressing or on a surface) has no collider.

- `blocker_size` is the authored footprint used to **place** a prop (cells reserved, room fitting). It is unchanged.
- `collider_size` is the box the player **walks into**, derived from the model: the union of every variant's bounding box,
  padded 4 cm, snapped up to 5 cm (`src/config/prop_collider_sizes.gd`, generated by `tools/props/emit_collider_sizes.gd`;
  rerun it after changing a model). A few props are capped or kept authored where the model overhangs what you hit
  (restaurant table balloons, beach umbrella canopy). This removed the invisible walls (bookcase 1.5 m wide for an 0.88 m
  model, beach chair 1.2 m deep for 0.38) and the walk-into-the-model cases (beds, server racks, vending machines).
- Hunters path on the 2D grid from the authored footprint cells; they have no physical collider, so they cannot get stuck
  on the box difference (see `docs/HUNTER_PATHING.md`).

## Rules the generator enforces (`PlacementClearance`, every chunk, deterministic)

1. **Door keep-clear zone.** No solid prop may reach into the 1.5 m either side of a room door, across the door's width.
2. **Walk-through.** From the hall side of each door a capsule of radius 0.3 m (player 0.2 plus a 10 cm margin) must reach
   the door's inside and every standable point of the room; a prop that blocks the door or cuts off 1 m2 or more is removed,
   the most separating one first (up to 8 per door). Surface props whose desk was removed go with it.

It is seeded by nothing but the chunk's own contents, so host and guests compute the same result. Cost: about 6 ms per
generated chunk (raster at 0.5 m, props stamped as expanded boxes). It removed about 2 % of placed props on the sampled
seeds (102 of 4,759 in 81 chunks), mostly desks and chairs sitting in a doorway line.

Switch: `StreamedRouteGenerator.CLEAR_WALKWAYS`.

## Validators

| Suite | What it checks |
| --- | --- |
| `collision` | `CollisionAudit` over real chunks of every district, 2 seeds: 0 keep-clear violations; every door walkable and every relay/battery/hiding spot reachable at radius 0.2 and 0.3; no cut-off floor pocket of 1.5 m2 or more at 0.3; the clean-up is deterministic |
| `colliders` | the derived table matches the models (not stale), no decor has a collider, generated chunks carry the derived box, non-solid props occupy no cells |
| `furniture` | chair and table sizes and colliders (DONE11) |
| `hunter_real` | hunters on the same real geometry (DONE7 follow-up) |

The audit works on `CollisionWorld` (`src/game/collision_world.gd`): the same boxes and walls as the 3D view, exact
point-to-rotated-box distance, a 0.25 m raster. `tools/look/collision_audit.gd` prints the counts and samples
(`-- SEEDS_CSV CHUNKS_PER_BIOME`); `tools/look/collider_audit.gd` lists blockers that disagree with their models.
Before the clean-up the audit found, on 285 doors of one seed: 30 props in keep-clear zones, 4 doors fully blocked
(a reception desk across an office-suite door) and 219 m2 of cut-off floor at radius 0.2; after it, 0 / 0 / 19 m2 of small
pockets (none of 1.5 m2).

At a 1 m lane (radius 0.4) the audit still finds one relay reachable only through a narrower gap in 733 doors; it is
reported, not enforced.

## SHOW COLLISION (admin, VISUAL tab)

Draws the solid props' colliders as red boxes and every door's keep-clear zone as a green rectangle, over the world with
no depth test. Persisted with the other admin visual toggles (`show_collision`), off at the end of a run.
Capture: `CAP_COLLISION=1 CAP_ONLY=restaurant godot --path . res://tools/look/capture.tscn -- OUTDIR TAG`
(`docs/collision_overlay_restaurant.png`).

## Not done

- Step-over rules for low props and crouching or hiding under tables: every solid prop is a full-height box, by design.
- A player-controller slide/step audit (getting caught on box edges); the box padding is the only mitigation.
- Wall-mounted and fixture colliders (lockers, hiding spots, vents) are not in `CollisionWorld`; their keep-clear is the
  existing one-cell exclusion around props.
- City: the generator does not exist yet; `CollisionWorld`/`PlacementClearance` work on any chunk with the same data
  (grid, solid props with `collider_size`, door props with `inward/outward/door_span`).
