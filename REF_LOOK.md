# Reference look (branch `ref-look`)

Restyle of the infinite route to match the Last Route 3D reference images
(`LastRoute-3D-refs/3.webp` .. `20.webp`): a dark, wet, liminal facility in
chunky pixel art, lit by amber lamps with teal terminals as the only cool accent.
Everything is procedural; there is no new imported art.

**Post-processing is OFF by default.** `RefLook.POST_EFFECTS` (src/look/ref_look.gd) gates
the pixel/dither/vignette/grade pass, glow, adjustments and heavy fog; set it to `true` to compare.
With it off the look comes from textures, real lamps, emissives and a dark ambient with very light fog.

## Where it lives (`src/look/`)

| File | Job |
| --- | --- |
| `ref_look.gd` | Palette, `Environment` (plum ambient, fog, filmic, glow), `install()` that adds the post layer, the region/room surface tables (`REGION_STYLE`, `ROOM_STYLE`), `grade_prop()` |
| `ref_pixel.gdshader` | Full-screen pass on a CanvasLayer *below* the HUD: block-resolution sampling (270 rows), 4x4 ordered dither into a 20-level ladder, plum/brass grade, desaturation, vignette |
| `ref_textures.gd` | Painted 32 px/m floors, walls (32x128, a whole storey), ceilings. Built into two `Texture2DArray`s |
| `ref_surface.gdshader` / `ref_surface_mesh.gd` | One merged mesh per chunk (floor+ceiling surface, wall surface) using the arrays; vertex colour carries roughness/metal/emission |
| `ref_decor.gd` | Per-chunk MultiMesh dressing: sconces, lanterns, chandeliers, caged ceiling lamps, teal terminals, frames, windows, pipes, cable swags, floor cables, planters, pedestal lanterns, additive floor pools, halos and glossy streak decals |
| `ref_streak.gdshader` | Reflection streak that always lies on the floor from a lamp's foot towards the camera (the "wet floor" read, since the Compatibility renderer has no SSR) |
| `lamp_field.gd` | Lends 12 real `OmniLight3D`s to the lamps nearest the camera (fades a light out before re-homing it) |

Hooks: `RefLook.install()` is called from `infinite_route_test.gd` and `main_3d.gd`
`_ready()`; `StreamedFacilityView3D` builds the chunk surface and decor (phases 2
and 31 of the incremental chunk build) and keeps its GridMap for collision only.
Decor is derived from chunk data and a seeded hash, so co-op peers build the same
lamps without sharing state.

## Region mapping

maze = industrial / offices / arcade, mansion = drawing room / hall / dining, hotel =
wallpapered corridors with runners, apartment = two-tone flats, beach = ivy garden
with a glass roof, gothic = vaulted brick/stone, carnival = star-carpet prize rooms,
prison = service bays with hazard striping, inferno = foundry plates with embers.

## Capture rig

```sh
godot --path . res://tools/look/capture.tscn -- OUTDIR TAG [SEED]
```

Environment: `CAP_ONLY=beach_cove,corridor_` (substring list), `CAP_LEVELS=-1,2`,
`CAP_YAWS=0,90`, `CAP_HUNTERS=aki,xivvy`, `CAP_HUD=1`, `CAP_FPS=1` (prints fps and
draw calls), `CAP_LIST=1`. Run `godot --headless --path . --import` first after
adding a `class_name` script.

## Known gaps

No fountain, hedge arches or two-flight grand staircase (the generator's
`grandFoyerStaircase` prop still renders, just in the new lighting); no hanging
cable bundles overhead beyond single swags; first-person held item is unchanged.

## Single level, taller rooms

The infinite route is one level (see INFINITE_WORLD.md). Ceiling heights come from
`RefLook.ceiling_height()`: corridor 3.7 m, room 4.6 m, hall 9.2 m, greenhouse 13.8 m.

## UI theme

`src/ui/ui_theme.gd` builds `src/ui/ref_theme.tres` (the project's default GUI theme):
Courier/monospace body, ink/crimson/amber from `Palette`, flat panels, crimson primary
buttons (`PrimaryButton`), Night Watch style `MenuItem` entries and `HudBarWarn/Safe/Low`
bar variations. Regenerate with
`godot --headless --path . --script tools/ui/build_theme.gd`. Reusable components:
`UiMenuList` (">>" marker, grey sub-labels, keyboard hint), `UiToast`, `UiHudBar`.
The title menu and pause screen keep the 2D Last Route button row. Screenshots: `UI_OPEN=report|settings|pause UI_SCENES=... tools/look/ui_capture.tscn`.
Menu screenshots: `UI_SCENES=res://scenes/main_menu.tscn godot --path . res://tools/look/ui_capture.tscn -- OUTDIR TAG`.

## Player model

`art/props-3d/characters/wooden_mannequin.glb` is generated by
`tools/characters/build_wooden_mannequin.py` (Blender, headless): 29 faceted segment
meshes, ball-joint rings, one warm-grey material, 1.70 m, relaxed arms-down rest pose;
clips idle, walk, sprint, chase, crouch, sit, lay, tpose; `R-Attachment`/`L-Attachment`
hand empties and eye empties. `PlayerModels.USE_WOODEN` (src/config/player_models.gd)
switches back to the legacy Minecraft-skin body (`player_legacy.tscn`).

## Blocky props, wide halls, archways

* `tools/props/` rebuilds 63 key furniture/fixture types (86 models) as Minecraft-style box
  models from one pixel atlas, same size/pivot as the originals; `PropModelCatalog.USE_BLOCKY_PROPS`
  switches them on, the original models stay as the fallback (see tools/props/README.md).
* Halls and corridors are three cells wide (`StreamedRouteGenerator.CORRIDOR_WIDTH`), with a carpet
  runner and pilasters; roomy rooms open onto the hall through three-cell archways ("arch"
  room-door props, drawn by `RefDecor._arches`), a few keep doors.
* Per-room seeded ceiling heights: corridors 3.0-3.6 m, rooms 3.2-4.4, halls 4.8-6.0, greenhouses
  6.4-7.5 (`RefLook.room_height`). Fixture density is budgeted per room (2-4 lamps, 1-2 windows, only in a few room kinds; none in corridors or the greenhouse, the outside is a void).

## Apartments, doors, props

* An apartment suite (17x17 sector) is four rooms sharing walls, no cross hall: bedroom (NW), bathroom (NE, en suite
  through the bedroom), living room (SW, entrance opens straight into it) and kitchen (SE), joined by single doors;
  the plan mirrors for the east-entrance sector. Furniture comes from the per-room templates (`apartment_living`: TV wall,
  couch facing it with coffee table and rug, armchair, dining set, clear entrance lane).
* Rooms are never smaller than 9x9 (sizes 9x9, 9x11, 11x9, 11x13 and larger), anchored at the sector's inner corner so the connector to the main hall is only a few cells long. Wide doors and arches are only for major rooms (halls, lobbies, greenhouses,
  very large rooms); small rooms and the end of a hallway get a single door.
* Props: `tools/props/` builds 434 blocky models (`blocky_props_extra.py` second batch, `blocky_props_variants.py` shape
  variants, `blocky_props_doors.py` doors and barrel). `capture.gd` has `CAP_AT=x,z` and `CAP_SUITE=apartment|office`.

## Hunters

A-Z and Void hunters use the blocky cube models in `assets/hunters_blocky/<id>/<id>.glb` (`HunterRoster.USE_BLOCKY`;
set it false for the older models, `USE_SPRITES` true for the flat cutouts). `HunterView3D` plays idle/walk/chase/sprint
(sprint while the runner is in direct sight and she has breath), stun, flight (Xivvy) and charge (Loxelle); Kathy (and
Umbriel disguised as her) carries, on a rare seeded roll (`HunterRoster.SCYTHE_CARRY_CHANCE` = 1/8, same on every peer),
`assets/props_3d/kathy_scythe.glb` in `R-HandProp` and plays `scythe_idle` / `scythe_walk`
(`scythe_walk` at 1.8x for a chase). The old glowing eye quads are gone (Kathy's beam stays). `HunterJumpscare` is OFF (`HunterJumpscare.ENABLED = false`: a catch goes straight to game over, no lunge or sting); when enabled it plays the model's
`jumpscare` clip under a faint red ambient so the dark cube textures read as a figure. Checks: `tools/look/hunter_check.tscn`
(HUNTERS=..., HUNTER_STATE=idle|walk|chase|search|stun|scream) and `tools/look/jumpscare_check.tscn` (JUMP_IDS=...).

## Light budget

A loaded scene had ~160 real `OmniLight3D` (interactable glows, ~60 fairy-light bulbs, checkpoint lights, braziers, plus the
8-light lamp pool); the Compatibility renderer drops lights past its caps in arbitrary order, so lamps were lit from the wrong
place or not at all. Now: `LampField` (1) only lends pool lights to lamps with a clear physics line to the camera (the pool
casts no shadows and used to glow through walls; lamps within 3 m are exempt, characters never block), and (2) keeps only the
6 nearest `LampField.make_budgeted()` lights within reach on (interactables, ceiling fairy-light bulbs, checkpoint light, braziers).
Visible real lights dropped from 99-162 to 2-7 per view; the only shadow-casting light is the flashlight spot.
`CAP_LIGHTS=1` / `CAP_LEGACY_LIGHTS=1` in `tools/look/capture.gd` print light counts and capture the old behaviour for comparison.
